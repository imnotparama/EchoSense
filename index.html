ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION

EchoSense System Architecture

⚡ Power Rail Separation

  • 5V USB Rail: High-current ERM vibration motor & buzzer via 2N2222 BJT (prevents MCU brownouts).
  • 3.3V Regulated: ESP32-S3, INMP441 MEMS microphone, and SSD1306 OLED display.
  • Decoupling: 100µF electrolytic + 0.1µF ceramic directly across INMP441 VDD/GND.

🎙️ INMP441 I2S Digital Audio

  • SCK (GPIO5): 512 kHz Bit Clock generated by ESP32 DMA.
  • WS (GPIO4): 16 kHz Left/Right Word Select framing clock.
  • SD (GPIO6): 24-bit serialized digital acoustic samples.

🧠 TinyML 1D-CNN Inference

  • On-Device: TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers (TFLM) running on Xtensa LX7 DSP.
  • Latency: 28-32 ms per inference window.
  • Classes: Fire Alarm (3.1 kHz), Doorbell (chime), Baby Cry (harmonic), Vehicle Horn (dual-tone).

📳 Haptic & Mobile BLE Feedback
